About Me

I am an Operations, Finance, and Data Professional based in Berlin with a primary focus on research and data analysis. My work spans industrial process support across major logistics networks in Germany, following my research assistantship at the University of Ghana Business School, which centered on applied research, data analytics, and teaching support to build expertise in finance, digital finance, machine learning, and AI.

I hold a Master’s degree in Finance and Investment and have developed advanced data analytics skills through hands-on projects and postgraduate-level coursework. In addition to my academic and technical work, I am a UEFA C-Licensed football coach, with experience in both community and competitive environments. I also have experience in logistics, commodity trading (including cocoa), and real estate operations.

I am interested in roles that combine research, analytics, and operations, particularly those that value interdisciplinary skills, evidence-based decision-making, and collaboration across technical and non-technical teams.
